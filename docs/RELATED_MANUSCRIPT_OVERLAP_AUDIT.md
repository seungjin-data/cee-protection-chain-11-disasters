# Related-manuscript overlap audit

## Manuscripts compared

1. Existing CEE manuscript COMMSENV-26-2919: *The Environmental Governance of AI Infrastructure: Energy Burden Redistribution across Territories, Sectors, and Time*.
2. Current disaster-governance manuscript: *Operational conversion of hazard information into human protective action across 11 major disasters*.

## Comparison

| Dimension | COMMSENV-26-2919 | Current manuscript | Substantive overlap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research question | How governmental responses to AI infrastructure redistribute energy-environmental burdens | Where event-time conversion from hazard information to protective human action becomes discontinuous | No |
| Primary data | 144 policy events across 25 jurisdictions | 11 historical disasters and event/locality documentary reconstructions | No |
| Unit of analysis | Policy event / jurisdiction | Disaster event and selected locality/sub-unit | No |
| Core constructs | Facilitation, Constraint, Steering; Spatial, Sectoral, Temporal redistribution | S1-S5 protection chain; P1/P2 mobilisation; operational discontinuity | No |
| Main quantitative analysis | Chi-square association and Cramer's V | Descriptive evidence-state counts; no inferential statistical model | No |
| Main outcome | Policy-mediated redistribution pattern | Documented conversion into protective action/human protection before local impact | No |
| Figures/tables/data reused | None identified | None identified | No |

## Adjudication

On the versions audited, the manuscripts share a broad environmental-governance domain and the same author, but they do not share the same dataset, unit of analysis, core construct, research question, statistical result, figure or substantive conclusion. They therefore do not constitute substantively overlapping manuscripts on the present record. A final overlap check should be repeated against the exact final manuscript before submission, but the current evidence does not support treating COMMSENV-26-2919 as a related/duplicate manuscript requiring overlap disclosure solely because it is under review at the same journal.
